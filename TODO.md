## Recommended additions, in priority order

The order favors groups where one display layout can cover several devices.

1. **Round AMOLED, 390 × 390 — 19 entries:** Approach S50; Approach S70 42mm;
   Descent G2; Descent Mk3 43mm / Mk3i 43mm; Epix Pro (Gen 2) 42mm;
   Forerunner 165 and 165 Music; Forerunner 170 and 170 Music;
   Forerunner 570 42mm; Forerunner 70; Instinct 3 AMOLED 45mm;
   Instinct Crossover AMOLED; MARQ (Gen 2) and MARQ (Gen 2) Aviator; Venu 3S;
   Venu 4 41mm; vívoactive 5 and 6.
1. **Round AMOLED, 454 × 454 and 466 × 466 — 15 entries:** Approach S70 47mm;
   D2 Mach 2 and Mach 2 Pro; Descent Mk3i 51mm; Epix Pro (Gen 2) 51mm /
   D2 Mach 1 Pro / tactix 7 AMOLED; fēnix 8 47/51mm / tactix 8 / quatix 8;
   fēnix 8 Pro; fēnix 9 47/51mm; fēnix 9 Pro 47mm and 51mm;
   Forerunner 570 47mm; Forerunner 965 and 970; Venu 3;
   Venu 4 45mm / D2 Air X15.
1. **Round AMOLED, 416 × 416 — 12 entries:** D2 Air X10; D2 Mach 1;
   Epix (Gen 2) / quatix 7 Sapphire; Epix Pro (Gen 2) 47mm / quatix 7 Pro;
   fēnix 8 43mm; fēnix 9 43mm and fēnix 9 Pro 43mm; fēnix E;
   Forerunner 265; Instinct 3 AMOLED 50mm; Venu 2 and Venu 2 Plus.
1. **MIP2, semi-octagon — 8 entries:** Descent G1 / G1 Solar;
   Instinct 2, 2S, and 2X; Instinct 3 Solar; Instinct Crossover;
   Instinct E 40mm and 45mm. This family spans several generations but needs a
   dedicated layout for the semi-octagon shape and monochrome screen.
   [Garmin lists the Instinct 2 as semi-octagon with a 2-color MIP display](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/compatible-devices/).
1. **Rectangular AMOLED — 3 entries:** Venu Sq 2, Venu Sq 2 Music, and Venu X1.
1. **Round AMOLED, 360 × 360 — 2 entries:** Forerunner 265S and Venu 2S.
1. **Round MIP8 — 1 entry:** Forerunner 55.

Total: **60 additional device entries with API 3.4 or later.**
